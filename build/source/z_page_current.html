One thing, five answers

A tail number, an airline or an aircraft type in — and the FAA's Service Difficulty Reports answer five ways: when, where on the aircraft, whose, what was found, and what it forced the crew to do. The same public corpus as the lookup desk.

The same public corpus as the SDR Desk lookup, read through a different lens. Codes are decoded from the FAA's own lookup tables; airline names come from the FAA's December 2006 cross-reference and can be stale, so check current ownership before publishing.
