# The five, measured in a browser

1. THE PAGE JUMPS 21px EVERY TIME THE AIM LINE CHANGES.
   Measured: the aim line is `display:none` when empty, so it has no box, and
   everything under it moves up and back on every hover. The original gives it
   `min-height:20px` and it never leaves the flow. A reader called this a shake
   and they are right.

2. THE TAB STRIP IS STICKY AND 231px TALL.
   Measured: `position:sticky; top:44px; z-index:40; height:231px`. The original
   does not make it sticky at all, and it is 104px. A third of the window is a
   bar the reports run under.

3. THE CASE SHEET CANNOT BE SCROLLED.
   Measured: `#case-box` is `position:fixed`, 1440x900, with 1521px of content
   inside it. In the original the overlay `#case-wrap` is what scrolls, and the
   card is a 900px-wide sheet centred in it with 32px of air above. Scrolling the
   card is not the same thing as scrolling the overlay, and it is why a reader
   cannot reach the bottom of a report.

4. THE LADDER PRINTS THE DESIGNATOR TWICE.
       now         Southwest Airlines Co (SWAA) (SWAA)
       should be   Southwest Airlines Co (SWAA)
   The menu label already ends in the designator. Strip a trailing parenthetical
