# Where it belongs

The page has a tab, `#p-aircraft`, labelled "Aircraft", in the group that
narrows to your selection. It is the natural home for everything keyed on a tail.

Every tail number in the record table is already clickable. Read how the page
makes values clickable and use the same mechanism, so a reader lands on the
dossier from a row rather than by typing.

# What to build

One dossier for one aircraft, in #p-aircraft, that reads:

  1. What this airframe is: make, model, how many reports, first to last.
  2. The stage framing sentence, verbatim from `framing.sentence`, with the
     in-flight and on-ground split beside it. This is the sentence that stops a
     reader treating a maintenance note as an in-flight event.
  3. What recurred: the repeat groups, with the hours between them, because a
     defect written up twice in nine hours is a different story from twice in
     nine months.
  4. The model's own summary, marked as written by a model, with `note` shown
     and `generated` respected: if it is false, say the summary was not written
     rather than showing an empty box.
  5. Every sentence in `cannot_show`, displayed, not hidden. They are the
     limits of the file and they are the most valuable thing on the page.
  6. The citation, and a link that downloads the CSV.

The reader must always be able to tell which words are the FAA's and which are
the model's. Mark the model's plainly.

# The checks, before you call done

  1. Open /z/?tail=N373UP, or whatever the page's own mechanism is, and the
     Aircraft tab shows the dossier for that tail with a non-zero report count.
  2. Clicking a tail number in the record table opens the same dossier for that
     tail.
  3. The framing sentence on screen equals `framing.sentence` from the endpoint,
     character for character.
  4. Every string in `cannot_show` appears on screen.
  5. The repeat groups show hours_between as a number a reader can read.
  6. The summary is labelled as written by a model, and when `generated` is
     false nothing pretends otherwise.
  7. The CSV link downloads and its first line begins with a # comment naming
     the source.
  8. A tail with no reports says so and does not throw.
  9. No page error at 1440 or 390, and nothing from phase B changes: the
     standing sentence still reads 219 reports at ?zone=ZONE+500&operator=CALA
     and the 1,757,827 sentence unfiltered, #vstrip at or under 130px, 18
     controls, 18 starter questions, 4 .rail, 16 section.panel.

Measure every one and put the numbers in the summary.

Do not undo the phone layout, the case sheet overlay, the record stepper or the
aria-labels. Prefix every new name.
